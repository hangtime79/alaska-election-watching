Projecting the 2026 Alaska Election Finish

Begich holds the House and Peltola takes the Senate, each by about 8,600 votes. This page projects the rest of the count and traces every ballot through Alaska's ranked-choice rounds.

Every number with a sign is the Democratic side minus the Republican side: negative is overperformance for the Republican, positive is overperformance for the Democrat. Red is Republican, blue is Democratic, purple is everyone else (Hill is an independent endorsed by the Democrats). My own tabulations of unofficial data.

Notes

  1. Counted ballots. The ballot-level data (the cast vote record, exported 4 Nov at about 1:20 p.m.) holds 204,530 ballots. The official Election Summary Report of 1:09 p.m. counts 215,912, with 400 of 401 precincts in. The difference, 215,912 − 204,530 == 11,382, is almost entirely rural election-day precincts: only 6 of the 106 precincts in HD37–40 are in the ballot-level data. The projection adds those ballots from the report's first choices, read through each race's own transfer pattern. The same lag happened in 2024, when 75% of rural election-day ballots arrived after 10 Nov.
  2. Sources. Alaska Division of Elections: Dominion cast vote record exports for 2022 (August special and November general), 2024 and 2026, tabulated with RCTab 2.1.0 under Alaska's ranked-choice rules; the Combined Ballot Report (4 Nov, 11:06 a.m.); the Election Summary Report (4 Nov, 1:09 p.m.). National figures: UF Election Lab and news projections. All tabulations are my own and unofficial.
