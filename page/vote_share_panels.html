Republican and Democratic vote share, by area, 2014–2026

Every statewide race since 2014, ranked within each area from the highest share down. Eight areas cover the whole state: the three cities, the cities together, the Bush, Mat-Su, the Kenai Peninsula and the rest. Bars share one scale (0–80%). A dashed line marks each panel's median. A highlighted row with ▲ or ▼ stands out from the rest of its panel: more than 2 robust standard deviations from the median (▲▲ / ▼▼ beyond 3).

Share == the side's votes / votes for all candidates in the race (minor candidates and write-ins in; blanks and overvotes out). All ballots: election day, absentee, early and questioned. 2022–2026 are first choices; 2026 is a partial count (Division of Elections precinct file, 4 Nov).

Areas, precinct by precinct, so they are the same places on both district maps: Anchorage == the Municipality (Eagle River, Chugiak, Eklutna, Peters Creek, Girdwood in); Fairbanks == the North Star Borough (North Pole, Eielson, Salcha in); Juneau == the City and Borough; Cities == the three together; Bush == every precinct of the old HD37–40 plus the off-road Interior villages (Arctic Village, Beaver, Fort Yukon, Stevens Village, Tanana, Venetie), followed by name into the new map; Mat-Su == the Matanuska-Susitna Borough; Kenai Peninsula == the Kenai Peninsula Borough (Seward, Seldovia and Tyonek in); Rest of state == everything else (Kodiak, Cordova, Valdez, the Southeast towns, the road-system Interior, the Denali Borough). The share of the statewide vote beside each panel title is from the 2024 House race (329,359 ballots); over 2014–2026 Anchorage ran 39–42%, Mat-Su 13–17% (growing), Fairbanks 12–13%, Rest of state 9–12%, Kenai 9–10%, Bush 5–8%, Juneau 5%.

Absentee, early and questioned votes are reported by House district. A district wholly inside one area gives them all to that area. In a district split between areas, each candidate's votes are divided in proportion to that candidate's election-day votes in each part. This assumes absentee voters in a split district vote like its election-day voters.

Outlier test, within each panel and recomputed for whatever races are shown: robust z == 0.6745 × (share − median) / MAD, where MAD is the median of the absolute distances from the median. A robust standard deviation is 1.4826 × MAD. Flag when |z| > 2; ▲▲ / ▼▼ when |z| > 3. Sampling error at these vote counts is a fraction of a point, so the test compares races with each other, not with chance.
