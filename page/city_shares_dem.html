Democrats in Alaska's cities, 2014–2026

Each statewide race's Democratic side as a share of the vote in Anchorage (with Eagle River and Chugiak), the Fairbanks North Star Borough (with North Pole) and Juneau. Highest first. Hover or tap a bar for each city and the count behind it.

Ballots
Democrat Independent or combined (I) Bold = Peltola

Share == the Democratic side's votes / votes for all candidates in the race (minor candidates and write-ins in; blank ballots and overvotes out). 2022–2026 are first choices.

Election day is the only vote type reported by precinct. 2020 election day is greyed out: in the pandemic year Democrats voted absentee, so election day ran far more Republican. Use the all-ballots view for 2020.

All ballots adds the absentee, early and questioned votes, which are reported by House district, so it covers only districts lying wholly inside a city.

(I): Walker/Mallott 2014 was an independent unity ticket with a Democratic running mate; Galvin, Gross and Hill were independents backed by the Democrats; Gara + Walker and Hill + Hafner add two candidates. 2026 is a partial count (Division of Elections precinct file, 4 Nov). Sources: OpenElections precinct files 2014–2020, RCTab cast vote records 2022–2024.
