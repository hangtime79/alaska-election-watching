Where several Republicans ran on one ranked-choice ballot, the bar adds them together (hatched). Hover or tap a bar for each city and the count behind it.

Share == the Republican side's votes / votes for all candidates in the race (minor candidates and write-ins in; blank ballots and overvotes out). 2022–2026 are first choices. Same cities, precincts and sources as the Democratic page.

Election day is the only vote type reported by precinct. 2020 election day is greyed out: in the pandemic year Democrats voted absentee, so election day ran far more Republican. Use the all-ballots view for 2020.

2016 Senate: Murkowski, with Miller (Libertarian) running to her right. 2022 Senate shows Murkowski on her own by default: she drew many Democrats, so her share isn't purely Republican, and Murkowski + Tshibaka together isn't a Republican side (the checkbox adds Tshibaka and the pair). 2026 is a partial count (Division of Elections precinct file, 4 Nov).
